Support

Support for the InkAds proof of concept

InkAds is a Singleton SD proof of concept for low-power e-paper advertising in shared indoor spaces. Use this page to find a contact path during the pilot.

About this page

A public help destination for the pilot.

InkAds is exploring a calmer display format for useful messages in physical places. This public support page is the help destination referenced by the InkAds Entra sign-in experience and related product surfaces.

During the proof of concept, support is provided by the Singleton SD team — not a dedicated ticketing portal. Reach out if you need help with device sign-in, venue or advertiser questions, or general product orientation.

How we can help

Practical questions during the pilot.

We can point you toward the right next step for sign-in, placement context, or a conversation about the concept.

  • Sign-in help

    Questions about accessing InkAds through the display device flow or related Microsoft Entra sign-in.

  • Pilot orientation

    Clarifying what the proof of concept covers for venues and advertisers — without unvalidated claims.

  • General contact

    Anything else about InkAds or Singleton SD can start with a short message via email or the contact page.
