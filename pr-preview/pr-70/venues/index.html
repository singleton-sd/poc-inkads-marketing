Why venues consider InkAds

Minimal installation

No mains power required at each screen — units fit where running permanent cabling would be inconvenient or expensive.

Remotely changeable content

Messaging updates on a schedule without a technician on site or a poster reprint.

Clean, quiet display

No bright video screen — a static, paper-like canvas that suits considered interiors.

Venue-owned messaging

Mix your own announcements with third-party advertising, on your terms.

What's involved

A light footprint for your team.

  • Setup

    A single battery-powered unit is mounted; no wiring or data drops.

  • Content

    You approve what appears in your space; venue messaging can run alongside advertiser content.

  • Ongoing

    Updates happen remotely on schedule — nothing to print, post, or replace on site.
