How it works

From message to place, in three steps.

The proof of concept focuses on the relationship between content, a low-power display device, and the shared space around it.

  1. 01

    Match

    A venue and a message are considered together. Location, dwell time, and audience guide which creative belongs where — not a generic ad slot filled at random.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Creative is composed for a static e-paper canvas — closer to designing a framed poster than a conventional animated digital screen. QR codes carry the call to action.

  3. 03

    Display

    The device presents the selected message within the venue's shared space, holding it without power draw until the next scheduled change.

Why e-paper

Not a smaller TV. A different kind of display.

Minimal installation

No mains power required at each screen, so units go where cabling would be inconvenient or expensive.

Remotely changeable

Content updates on a schedule, without printing or replacing physical posters.

Calm and quiet

A static, paper-like canvas with no bright video or motion — not intended to compete with animated signage.

Built for long dwell

The strength is low-power, static advertising that stays visible for long periods in places people pause.
