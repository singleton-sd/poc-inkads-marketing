Useful messages. Placed thoughtfully.

The idea

A calmer format for useful messages in physical places.

InkAds connects venue space with advertiser messages through e-paper displays designed for low power use — a considered alternative to adding another bright, animated screen. The concept targets units that need no mains power at each screen, with content changing remotely on a schedule.
