Use cases · built to adapt

Start specific. Think beyond one setting.

Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context, not the limit of the product idea. The same format could be explored wherever a calm, considered display belongs.

Initial focus

Bathrooms

An initial context for testing how a compact display fits into a private, high-dwell shared space — where a message is read closely, uninterrupted.

02

Pubs & hospitality

Potential placements across venues where people gather, pause, and make local decisions — near entries, waiting areas, and washrooms.

04

High-traffic spaces

Cafés, gyms, and other suitable physical settings where a static message can complement the environment.

A constraint, not a limitation

Not built to compete with video signage.

InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. The concept focuses on static advertising designed for low power use, intended to stay visible for long periods and update remotely — a fit for spaces where a bright screen would feel out of place.
