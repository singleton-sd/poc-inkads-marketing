E-paper · shared spaces

Useful messages. Placed thoughtfully.

InkAds is exploring a low-power e-paper advertising network that connects venues and advertisers through calm, context-aware displays.

Two distinct paths. One considered network.

The idea

A calmer format for useful messages in physical places.

InkAds is a proof of concept for connecting venue space with advertiser messages through low-power e-paper displays. It is being explored as an alternative to adding another bright, animated screen.
