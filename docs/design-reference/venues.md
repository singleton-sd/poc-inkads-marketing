## Why venues consider InkAds (white band)

- **Minimal installation** — No mains power required at each screen — units fit where running permanent cabling would be inconvenient or expensive.
- **Remotely changeable content** — Messaging updates on a schedule without a technician on site or a poster reprint.
- **Clean, quiet display** — No bright video screen — a static, paper-like canvas that suits considered interiors.
- **Venue-owned messaging, plus revenue** — Mix your own announcements with third-party advertising, on your terms.
