# About

## Intro

**Eyebrow:** About

**H1:** A proof of concept, built with care.

**Lead:** InkAds is a proof of concept for connecting venue space with advertiser messages through low-power e-paper displays — developed by Singleton SD.

---

## Two columns

**The constraint** — InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. Its strength is low-power, static advertising that can remain visible for long periods and update remotely — a calmer format for places where a bright screen would feel out of place.

**Where it started** — Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context: a private, high-dwell space to learn how a compact display earns its place before exploring pubs, shopping centres, and other high-traffic settings.
