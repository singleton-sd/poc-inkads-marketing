# Pricing

## Intro

**Eyebrow:** Pricing

**H1:** Still in proof-of-concept.

**Lead:** InkAds is being tested with a small number of venues and advertisers before we set standard pricing. Talk to us and we'll scope a pilot around your space or campaign.

---

## Two pilots

**For venues** (grey border)

- Title: Pilot install
- A single display, mounted with no cabling, running venue and partner content for an agreed pilot period. Scoped per space — no published rate card yet.
- Link: Request pilot pricing → Contact (gold)

**For advertisers** (purple border `#8432ff`)

- Title: Placement pilot
- A scheduled creative run across a small set of venues matched to your audience, with QR tracking. Priced with you as we test the model.
- Link: Request placement pricing → Contact (purple)

---

## Note (white band, centered)

Standard plans and self-serve pricing will follow as InkAds moves beyond the pilot stage. Have thoughts on what a fair model looks like? We'd like to hear them.
