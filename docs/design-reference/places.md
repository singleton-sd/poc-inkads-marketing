# Use cases

## Intro

**Eyebrow:** Use cases · built to adapt

**H1:** Start specific. Think beyond one setting.

**Lead:** Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context, not the limit of the product idea. The same format could be explored wherever a calm, considered display belongs.

---

## Use-case cards (2×2)

1. **Bathrooms** — _Initial focus_ (gold border). An initial context for testing how a compact display fits into a private, high-dwell shared space — where a message is read closely, uninterrupted.
2. **Pubs & hospitality** — Potential placements across venues where people gather, pause, and make local decisions — near entries, waiting areas, and washrooms.
3. **Shopping centres** — A format to explore across varied retail environments and shared amenities, from concourses to family rooms.
4. **High-traffic spaces** — Cafés, gyms, and other suitable physical settings where a static message can complement the environment.

---

## A constraint, not a limitation (white band)

Two columns: image slot `places-visual` + copy.

**H2:** Not built to compete with video signage.

**Body:** InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. Its strength is low-power, static advertising that can remain visible for long periods and update remotely — a fit for spaces where a bright screen would feel out of place.
