## Why advertisers consider InkAds (white band)

Eyebrow purple.

- **Targeted by venue & location** — Choose placements by the character of the space — not just footfall, but context.
- **Scheduled campaign changes** — Swap creative remotely without printing or physically replacing posters.
- **QR-code calls to action** — A static canvas still connects to a live destination — a page, an offer, a booking.
- **Proof-of-display telemetry** — Device telemetry is on the roadmap, to confirm creative was live where and when planned.

---

## Designing for e-paper

**H2:** Compose for a poster, not a video screen.

**Body:** InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. Creative works best as a single, still composition — high contrast, clear type, and a QR code — designed to reward a longer look rather than a passing glance.
