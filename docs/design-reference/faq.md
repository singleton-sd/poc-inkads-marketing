# FAQ

**Eyebrow:** FAQ

**H1:** Common questions.

Single column, max-width 900px. Q 19px / 500, A 15px / 300 / `#9da1a6`.

## Q: Is InkAds trying to replace video digital signage?

No. InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. Its strength is low-power, static advertising that can remain visible for long periods and update remotely.

## Q: Do displays need mains power or a network cable?

No mains power is required at each screen, which is what makes InkAds suited to locations where running permanent cabling is inconvenient or expensive.

## Q: How does content get updated?

Campaigns and venue messaging change remotely on a schedule, without printing or physically replacing a poster.

## Q: Where is InkAds being tested first?

Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context — a private, high-dwell shared space — not the limit of the idea. Pubs, shopping centres, cafés, gyms, and other high-traffic indoor locations are all in scope.

## Q: Can I track whether my creative was actually displayed?

Proof-of-display and device telemetry is on the roadmap, alongside QR-code calls to action already supported today.

## Q: What does it cost?

InkAds is still in proof-of-concept, so pricing is scoped per pilot with venues and advertisers. See Pricing for details.
