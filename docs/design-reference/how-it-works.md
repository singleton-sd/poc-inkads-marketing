# How it works

## Intro

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**H1:** From message to place, in three steps.

**Lead:** The proof of concept focuses on the relationship between content, a low-power display device, and the shared space around it.

Layout: single column, max-width 900px, left-aligned. H1 56px.

---

## Three steps

Numbered list, 120px number column (`#292c30`) + copy. Gold step titles.

1. **Match** — A venue and a message are considered together. Location, dwell time, and audience guide which creative belongs where — not a generic ad slot filled at random.
2. **Prepare** — Creative is composed for a static e-paper canvas — closer to designing a framed poster than a conventional animated digital screen. QR codes carry the call to action.
3. **Display** — The device presents the selected message within the venue's shared space, holding it without power draw until the next scheduled change.

---

## Why e-paper (white band)

**H2:** Not a smaller TV. A different kind of display.

Two-by-two grid:

- **Minimal installation** — No mains power required at each screen, so units go where cabling would be inconvenient or expensive.
- **Remotely changeable** — Content updates on a schedule, without printing or replacing physical posters.
- **Calm and quiet** — A static, paper-like canvas with no bright video or motion — not intended to compete with animated signage.
- **Built for long dwell** — The strength is low-power, static advertising that stays visible for long periods in places people pause.
