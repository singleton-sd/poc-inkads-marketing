This Privacy Policy describes how Singleton SD operates the InkAds proof-of-concept
marketing site at `inkads.poc.singletonsd.com`.

## What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the fields you submit: name, venue or
company name, email address, how you identify (venue, advertiser, or other), and
your message.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address and message content.

## How we use it

We use contact submissions to respond to questions about the InkAds proof of
concept. We do not sell contact details or use them for unrelated marketing
without your consent.

## How submissions are delivered

Contact form messages are sent through Singleton SD's shared PostKit email
service and delivered to the InkAds support inbox configured for this site. The
form posts to PostKit's `/contact` endpoint from your browser; PostKit applies
rate limiting and routes the message to our inbox. Email delivery uses Forward
Email as configured for Singleton SD PoC domains.

## Hosting and processors

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Analytics

This proof-of-concept site does not currently use third-party analytics on the
public pages. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before
they are enabled.
