InkAds is exploring a calmer display format for useful messages in physical
places. This public support page is the help destination referenced by the
InkAds Entra sign-in experience and related product surfaces.

During the proof of concept, support is provided by the Singleton SD team — not
a dedicated ticketing portal. Reach out if you need help with device sign-in,
venue or advertiser questions, or general product orientation.
