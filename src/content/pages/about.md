  - title: The constraint
    body: >-
      InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. Its
      strength is low-power, static advertising that can remain visible for long
      periods and update remotely — a calmer format for places where a bright
      screen would feel out of place.
  - title: Where it started
    body: >-
      Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context: a private, high-dwell
      space to learn how a compact display earns its place before exploring pubs,
      shopping centres, and other high-traffic settings.
