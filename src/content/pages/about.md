  - title: The constraint
    body: >-
      InkAds is not intended to compete with animated LCD or video signage. The
      proof of concept aims for low-power, static advertising that stays visible
      for longer periods and can be updated remotely — a calmer format for places
      where a bright screen would feel out of place.
  - title: Where it started
    body: >-
      Bathrooms are the initial proof-of-concept context: a private space where
      people pause, used to learn how a compact display earns its place before
      exploring pubs, shopping centres, and other indoor settings.
